### 🍣 What we build

[**Sushi**](https://github.com/getsushi/sushi) is a desktop app for job seekers. It reads your CV, finds matching roles directly on companies' own careers pages, and fills in each application in a real browser. Every form is left for you to check before anything is sent.

- **Finds jobs early**: reads 26,000+ companies' careers pages through their public ATS APIs
- **Explains every match** with a score and the reasons behind it
- **Fills real forms**: dropdowns, typeaheads, dates, uploads and follow-up questions
- **Learns from you**: every answer you correct is remembered for the next form
- **Private by design**: no account, no cloud, and your email and phone number are never sent to the model
